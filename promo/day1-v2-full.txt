Hey everyone! Welcome to Day 1 of building in public.

Today, I want to show you something I've been working on.

It's an interactive Koi Fish wallpaper.

Just click anywhere on the water to drop some fish food, and watch the koi swim towards it and start eating. If the food lands on a lotus leaf, the leaves actually move and react to it.

I've also added a lot of little details to make the pond feel alive: realistic water ripples, tiny air bubbles, soft shadows from the lotus flowers, and much more.

You can customize the experience, too.

Change the number of fish in the pond, adjust the frame rate up to 144 FPS, and choose the rendering quality, from low all the way up to Ultra.

But this is just the beginning.

I'm thinking of adding a day-and-night cycle, so you can experience the pond under moonlight, along with a rainy weather effect, where raindrops create ripples across the water.
